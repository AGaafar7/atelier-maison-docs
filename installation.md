# Installation

## Before you begin

You need:

- An active Shopify store.
- Access to the Shopify admin.
- The Atelier Maison theme ZIP package supplied with your purchase.

## Upload the theme

1. Sign in to your Shopify admin.
2. Open **Online Store → Themes**.
3. In the Theme library area, select **Add theme → Upload zip file**.
4. Choose the Atelier Maison ZIP package.
5. Wait for Shopify to finish uploading the theme.
6. Select **Customize** to open the theme editor.

Uploading adds the theme to your library. It does not automatically publish it to your storefront.

## Preview before publishing

1. Open **Online Store → Themes**.
2. Find Atelier Maison in your theme library.
3. Select **Preview** to inspect the storefront.
4. Open the theme editor to configure your store.
5. Publish only when you have completed your setup and testing.

## Configure your store content

Before launch, add your products, product images, collections, navigation menus, pages, policies, and social links in Shopify admin.
