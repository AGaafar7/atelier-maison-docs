# Templates and storefront features

Atelier Maison includes the following templates:

| Template | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Home | Main storefront landing page |
| Product | Product details and purchase |
| Collection | Product listing and collection browsing |
| List collections | Collection directory |
| Cart | Shopping cart |
| Search | Store search results |
| Blog | Blog article listing |
| Article | Individual blog article |
| Page | Standard content page |
| 404 | Not-found page |
| Password | Password-protected storefront |
| Gift card | Gift card presentation |

## Product page

The product template supports product information and purchasing through Shopify's product form. The section settings include vendor visibility and the dynamic checkout button.

Ensure products have accurate descriptions, prices, images, inventory, and variant information in Shopify admin.

## Collection page

The collection section includes controls for:

- Collection eyebrow, description, and image visibility.
- Filters and sorting.
- Products per page.
- Desktop grid columns.
- Quick add on product cards.
- Quick add and choose-options labels.

Defaults are 16 products per page and 4 desktop columns. Products per page can be adjusted from 8 to 32 in increments of 4; desktop columns can be adjusted from 2 to 5.

Available filters depend on the filters configured for your store.

## Cart

Customers can review items, update quantities, and proceed to checkout. The theme also includes an AJAX cart drawer.

## Search

The search page supports product, article, and page results, with configurable results per page. The default is 16, adjustable from 8 to 32 in increments of 4.

Predictive search provides suggestions while customers type.

## Blog and content pages

Create and manage blog posts and pages in Shopify admin. The theme supplies templates for blog listings, articles, and standard pages.
